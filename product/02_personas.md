# Personas

## 1. Priya, project manager (primary)
*Delivery PM in a 300-person IT services firm. Runs 2–3 projects of £100k–£2m.*

- **Job to be done:** "When I'm asked to commit to a budget and date, I want to know how likely they are, so I don't sign up to something I'll miss."
- **Today:** MS Project for the plan, Excel for costs, "+10%" contingency. Has been burned by a date set by the sponsor.
- **Pains:** can't explain *why* she needs more contingency; risk register is a list that never changes a number.
- **Success looks like:** a one-page answer she can put in front of a sponsor, and a way to show what a mitigation buys.
- **Must-haves:** works from her existing WBS spreadsheet; under 10 minutes to a first answer; no install.

## 2. Tom, bid manager (secondary)
*Prices fixed-price engineering and consultancy bids.*

- **Job to be done:** "When I price a fixed-price bid, I want a risk allowance I can defend to the commercial director, so we win without giving away margin."
- **Pains:** contingency is negotiated, not calculated; losing bids by over-pricing and losing money by under-pricing.
- **Success looks like:** P50 / P80 / P90 prices side by side, and the cost of each risk in the bid.
- **Must-haves:** cost-only mode (often no schedule at bid stage); confidence-level switch; export for the bid pack.

## 3. Aisha, PMO lead (buyer / influencer)
*Runs a portfolio office of 25 projects. Sets the governance standards.*

- **Job to be done:** "When projects ask for contingency, I want a consistent method across the portfolio, so approvals are fair and auditable."
- **Pains:** every PM uses a different spreadsheet; no audit trail of assumptions.
- **Success looks like:** the same method and report format for every project; saved assumptions in the project file.
- **Must-haves:** a standard report; assumptions visible; data stays inside the organisation.

## Anti-persona
**Cost engineers on £100m+ capital programmes** already use Primavera Risk Analysis or Safran with a dedicated analyst. RiskSim is not trying to replace that workflow.
