# Competitive landscape

| Option | Typical user | Strengths | Weaknesses for our personas |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Spreadsheet + "add 10%"** | Most PMs | Free, familiar | No ranges, no odds, no risk ranking; contingency is negotiated, not calculated |
| **@RISK (Lumivero)**, Excel add-in | Analysts, finance | Powerful, flexible | Needs model-building skill; subscription priced per seat (Professional reported at €2,900/year¹) |
| **Oracle Primavera Risk Analysis** | Large capital programmes | Integrates with P6 schedules; industry standard for schedule risk | Enterprise sale (price on request²); needs a specialist; overkill below £10m |
| **Safran Risk** | Large engineering programmes | Integrated cost and schedule risk | Enterprise tool and workflow |
| **RiskSim** | PMs, bid teams, PMOs | Browser, no install, minutes to first answer, plain-English output, risk ranking by budget/date impact, scenarios | Early MVP: no schedule-tool integration, no portfolio view, no team features |

**Positioning:** *"The risk analysis a specialist would do, for projects that will never have a specialist."*

**Where we win:** £100k–£10m projects where the alternative is a spreadsheet. Speed and clarity beat depth.
**Where we don't compete:** programmes that already employ a risk analyst and run Primavera or Safran.
