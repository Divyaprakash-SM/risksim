# Pricing and go-to-market (hypotheses to test)

These are **hypotheses**, not decisions. Each has a test.

## Pricing hypothesis
| Tier | Price (hypothesis) | For | Includes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | £0 | Individual PMs, students | Full simulation, 1 project at a time, watermark on board report |
| Pro | ~£25 per user per month | PMs, bid managers | No watermark, scenarios, Excel export, MS Project import |
| Team | ~£150 per month for 10 users | PMOs | Portfolio view, shared templates, standard report branding |

**Anchor:** well below specialist tools (e.g. @RISK Professional at a reported €2,900/year per seat) and close to a typical project-management SaaS seat.

**Test:** a pricing page with three tiers and a "join the waiting list" button per tier. Measure click-through by tier across ~200 visitors before building billing.

## Go-to-market hypothesis
1. **Community-led:** PMI chapters, APM branches and LinkedIn PMO groups. A free "is your contingency enough?" check using the sample.
2. **Bid teams:** short case study: "how much margin a P80 price would have protected on the last 5 bids".
3. **Education:** free for universities teaching project risk (it began as an MSc dissertation).

## Riskiest assumptions
| Assumption | How we'd know it's wrong | Test |
|---|---|---|
| PMs want odds, not a single number | Users ignore P-levels and screenshot only the base estimate | Usability test: ask users to explain the headline back |
| Sponsors accept a simulated contingency | Sponsors overrule it with "+10%" | Pilot with 3 PMOs; track whether recommended contingency is approved |
| Not storing data is a feature, not a bug | Users ask for cloud save in the first session | Count requests in testing and feedback |
